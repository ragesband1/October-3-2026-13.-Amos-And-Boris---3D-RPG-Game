13. Amos And Boris

Written by Newtwart Stakeham | March 4 2017

Once upon a time, there was a giant whale named Boris and a brave mouse named Amos. They were the truest best friends.

Suddenly, beneath the watersides near a boat, a massive hurricane struck! A ferocious tidal wave separated Amos and Boris in the dark ocean depths.

On the second day, Boris fought through rogue waves to rescue Amos. Joined by a heroic Elephant in this tragic ordeal, they swore never to let go of each other.

"Moral Lesson: Real best friends never betray each other—they save one another from the most tragic experiences."
